# o1 Mobile

> Trade spot tokens across supported chains, follow other traders, and manage a mobile wallet in o1 Mobile.

o1 Mobile is a social trading app. You fund one USDC balance on Base, buy and sell tokens on supported chains, and use Market, Feed, and Friends to decide what to look at next. You confirm every swap yourself.

The app can be switched between English and Chinese. This documentation is in English.

## Five places to start

The tab bar is ordered Market, Search, Feed, Friends, and Profile.

> [card] [Market](https://docs.o1.exchange/mobile/market-and-social.md)
>
> Browse tokens, your watchlist, and short leaderboard lists.

> [card] [Search](https://docs.o1.exchange/mobile/market-and-social.md)
>
> Find a token or a person, then open the result.

> [card] [Feed](https://docs.o1.exchange/mobile/social.md)
>
> See buys, sells, and theses from the community or from friends.

> [card] [Friends](https://docs.o1.exchange/mobile/social.md)
>
> Follow traders and open the full leaderboard.

> [card] [Profile](https://docs.o1.exchange/mobile/wallet-and-security.md)
>
> Review your balance, positions, and wallet controls.

> [card] [Networks](https://docs.o1.exchange/mobile/chains.md)
>
> See which chains you can trade on, and where USDC deposits belong.

## Mobile and the website

o1 Mobile is for spot swaps, the supported chains, and the social tools in the app. Limit, TWAP, and sniper orders described in the Terminal documentation are not part of the mobile tab bar or the mobile trade sheet. Token creation stays on o1 Launchpad.

Use this Mobile section for the app. Use the Terminal and Launchpad sections for those products.
