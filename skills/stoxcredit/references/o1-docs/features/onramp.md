# Onramp Fiat to Crypto

o1 Terminal provides integrated fiat onramp solutions powered by Coinbase, allowing you to convert traditional currencies (USD, EUR, etc.) directly into cryptocurrency without leaving the platform.

### Supported Payment Methods

> [card] Credit/Debit Cards
>
> Instant purchases with major credit and debit cards

> [card] Bank Transfers
>
> Lower fees with ACH and wire transfer options

> [card] Digital Wallets
>
> Support for Apple Pay, Google Pay, and other digital wallets
