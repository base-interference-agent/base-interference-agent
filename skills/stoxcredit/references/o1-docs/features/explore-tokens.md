# Explore Tokens

o1 Terminal provides comprehensive token exploration tools to help you discover new opportunities, research projects, and make informed trading decisions across multiple blockchain networks.

### Token Discovery Features

> [card] Trending Tokens
>
> Discover the most popular and trending tokens based on trading volume and community interest

> [card] New Listings
>
> Stay updated with newly listed tokens and early-stage opportunities

> [card] Top Gainers/Losers
>
> Track the best and worst performing tokens over various time periods

> [card] High Volume
>
> Find tokens with exceptional trading volume and liquidity

#### Filter Tokens by Your Criteria

![Pulse page filter options](https://mintcdn.com/o1exchange/h74U8tuW1ielvxuG/images/pulse-filter.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=h74U8tuW1ielvxuG\&q=85\&s=1ae57da7f27bba79961adba681fe4a51)
