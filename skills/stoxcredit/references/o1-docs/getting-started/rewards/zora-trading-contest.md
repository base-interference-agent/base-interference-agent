# Zora Trading Contest

> Eight-week trading contest for Zora Creator and Content Coins

## Contest Overview

The first Zora Trading Contest is an eight-week competitive campaign with a total reward pool of 2,500,000 \$ZORA tokens.

### Campaign Duration

> [card] Contest Period
>
> **November 3, 2025 - December 27, 2025**
>
> 8 consecutive weekly periods, resetting every Monday at 00:00 SGT

## Rewards Pool

> [card] Total Prize Pool
>
> **2,500,000 \$ZORA**
>
> Allocated as 312,500 \$ZORA per weekly window across 8 weeks

## Eligibility Requirements

> [!NOTE]
>
> Only trades executed directly through o1 Terminal count toward eligibility. External trades (including after wallet export) are not eligible.

## Eligible Trading Activity

The contest tracks bona fide trades of:

* **Zora Creator Coins**
* **Zora Content Coins**

All trades must be executed via the o1 Terminal platform to qualify for leaderboard rankings.

>
> Rewards will be distributed within 7 days of the weekly volume window.
>
