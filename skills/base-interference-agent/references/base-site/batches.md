Overview

## What Is Base Batches?

Base Batches is the official Base accelerator run by the Base Ecosystem Fund. Launched in 2025, the program runs twice a year for a small group of high-quality early-stage teams building on Base. Batch 004 marks our fourth cohort. We’re looking for companies in trading, payments, agents, financing, and asset issuance that can help grow the onchain economy on Base.

Eligibility

## Who Should Apply

### Early Stage

Pre-product to post-MVP teams that have not yet raised a formal Seed round.

### Strong Conviction

Founders with clear insight and a credible path to building something meaningful.

### Base First

Teams committed to Base as their default implementation and network of choice.

## Do I need to already be building on Base?

You do not need to be fully live on Base today, but your company should be focused on Base as its primary chain and default network of choice.

## What kinds of teams are you looking for?

We're looking for early-stage teams building products that can grow the onchain economy on Base, especially across trading, payments, agents, financing, and asset issuance.

## Does applying guarantee funding?

No. Applying creates no obligation on Base, Coinbase, or the Base Ecosystem Fund to invest. All investments are subject to diligence, compliance review, and execution of definitive documents.

## Is this a grant program?

No. Batch 004 is built around direct investment from the Base Ecosystem Fund rather than grants. Selected teams will be offered an investment from the Base Ecosystem Fund on a standard Simple Agreement for Future Equity (SAFE). Specific terms, including the valuation cap, are shared with teams at the offer stage and are subject to the fund’s diligence review. Accepting the investment is a condition of joining the program.

## How many teams will be selected?

The program is designed around roughly 10 teams, with flexibility to expand if more teams meet the bar for participation.

## Is the program in person?

The core program is virtual. Selected teams will present in-person at Demo Day in New York.

Join an 8-week program with hands-on support from the Base Ecosystem team to refine your product, find product-market fit, and raise capital. Selected teams will receive an offer of $100K investment from the Base Ecosystem Fund.

Base is where the next generation of financial products get built. Base Batches funds and supports the founders building towards that future. We can't wait to see what you're working on.
