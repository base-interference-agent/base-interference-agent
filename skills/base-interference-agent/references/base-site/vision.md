# Vision

> A global economy that increases innovation, creativity, and freedom.

URL: https://www.base.org/vision

Trading

## Building global markets.

Every asset on earth is coming onchain, and every market is going 24/7. Base is building those global markets onchain — the trusted infrastructure and app where any asset can be tokenized, any market traded, and anyone can participate.

- Upgrade the chain for markets: purpose-built market infrastructure at the chain level, new ERCs for smart accounts and tokens, and scaling to maintain sub-second settlement at sub-cent cost
- Bring every major asset and market to Base: equities, commodities, and every major asset class, in every market structure: native issuance, tokenization, predictions, perpetuals, spot
- Make Base App the trusted place to trade everything, available everywhere: trade millions of assets 24/7, discover what's trending from friends and top traders, and grow your wealth

Payments

## Scaling payments and stablecoins.

$17T in volume on Base last year and growing. Base is building the rails to make stablecoin payments low-cost, instant, private, and available everywhere.

- Upgrade the chain for payments: privacy primitives, native account abstraction and stablecoin gas payments, and protocol-level support for memos, policies, and rewards
- Create the most liquid markets for stablecoins: quality stablecoins for every currency in the world with easy to access liquidity for trading, borrowing, and lending
- Make Base App the easiest way to save, spend, and pay anyone: earn yield on your USDC, spend it anywhere, and borrow against your wealth

Builders

## Be the home for builders.

Builders build apps and markets, bring new demand, and grow the economy with their ingenuity and hard work. In 2026, Base is building the systems that help every builder (human or agent) spin this flywheel faster.

- Upgrade the chain for agents: agent-native smart accounts, docs, & CLI+MCP access and standards like x402 to help agents do anything with money in a fast, easy, trusted way
- Grow a based builder community: Base Batches, builder councils, and ecosystem programs that help the best teams and builders find their footing, get funded, and build scalable businesses
- Measure, incentivize, and grow the economy: ERC-8021 builder codes, analytics dashboards, liquidity incentives, and growth programs that reward those who bring more users and volume

## We win together

We share this because we’ve always believed in building in the open: sharing where we’re headed, listening to our community, and evolving based on what we learn together. The global economy belongs to all of us. Let’s build.
