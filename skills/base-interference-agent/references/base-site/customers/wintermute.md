 Wintermute is a London-based algorithmic trading firm that makes markets in digital assets on centralized and decentralized exchanges. Between January and September 2026 it traded more than $70 billion on Base. For a bank or fund asking whether onchain markets are deep enough to trade in, that is the trading activity already there.

 What Wintermute does
 Wintermute quotes prices to buy and sell hundreds of tokens across 70 venues, and takes on the price risk other traders don’t want to hold. Evgeny Gaevoy founded the firm in 2017.

 How it trades on Base
 Wintermute trades on Base through a smart contract it deployed itself. The goal is tight spreads and prices in line with centralized exchanges, for the solvers, request-for-quote (RFQ) platforms and liquidation engines that route and settle trades on Base.

 “ If there is no liquidity in DeFi, nobody is going to use it. If prices are disconnected from reality, from centralized exchanges, nobody is going to use it. That’s where we step in. ”
 Evgeny Gaevoy
 Founder and CEO, Wintermute

 Why Base
 Coinbase brings its own customers onchain. For Wintermute, that is “the user base that basically allows you to test new primitives, new products,” with fast feedback on what works. Coinbase is also investing to bring more people into DeFi. For Wintermute, that investment in the network’s growth matters.
 Moving funds between Coinbase and Base is simple, and USDC is easy to get and easy to use. “USDC is just really the lifeblood of DeFi, not just on Base,” Evgeny says.

 What it means for institutions
 Wintermute is an active market maker on Base. Evgeny sees blockchains as an alternative to a system where “you can be excluded from the system at any point of time.” Wintermute is “heavily invested in DeFi to give it a fighting chance,” he says, “and liquidity is ultimately how we do it.”
