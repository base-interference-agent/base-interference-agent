### **Everything you do has value. It’s time you earned from it.**

At Base, our mission is to build a global economy that increases innovation, creativity, and freedom.

We started by building the rails to move money globally for less than a cent and in less than a second. Now we’re focused on helping billions of people make it in this new economy with the Base App.

The Base App is an everything app that brings together social, trading, payments, app discovery, and earning into one place.

It’s built on a very simple new reality: In the app, everything is tokenized and tradable — protocols, apps, creators, posts and more — and it’s all directly valued in the free, open market. By putting value directly into your hands, we’re creating a market where everyone shares in the upside and traders can back the next generation of builders and creators.

**Starting today, the Base App is available to everyone in more than 140 countries.**

### **The "For You" Page of the New Onchain Economy**

When you open the app, the feed may look familiar, but it behaves differently – because it’s a feed that can make you money. It's a new kind of social feed built on open protocols like Zora and Farcaster where everything you create or build has value.

- **Discover the internet’s best assets:** Find new creators, trending tokens, viral content, or new communities forming in real time. Everything single thing you see is tokenized and tradeable, unlocking a new way of earning.
- **Be early, trade instantly.** See what your friends are trading and copy their moves so you don’t miss out. Set up quick buy to trade preset amounts of any asset just by double-tapping a post.
- **Trade millions of assets.** Base is becoming the best place to trade every single asset on the market, from Bitcoin, to Solana tokens, to tokenized equities and perpetual futures.
- **Follow the alpha:** Track top traders, turn on notifications when they trade, and discover new trending coins by market cap, liquidity, or volume.

We’ve completely reimagined what it looks like to trade, stripping away the complexity of traditional exchanges and replacing it with speed and social context directly in your feed.

**Countless ways to make money**

In the new global economy, everyone can have the freedom to participate on their own terms. With Base App, this means earning through your content, trading activity, and more everyday actions.

- **Earn when people interact with your posts.** Get a portion of the transaction fees immediately when people trade your content.
- **Trade with friends and agents in encrypted chat:** Share trades, send crypto, and interact with AI agents to manage your portfolio or execute trades, all through encrypted DMs.
- **Get rewarded from games, tools, and more:** Discover and play games to earn or access DeFi apps to stake and lend crypto. No downloads or switching tabs required.
- **Grow your money:** Earn up to **3.35% APY** just by holding USDC in the app.

### **Earn from anything you create.**

### The shift to onchain social means more opportunities for you to build a community that earns with your success.

- **Grow with your creator coin**: Creator coins let creators monetize their value directly, not just through ads, merch, or brand deals. These coins create a direct economic relationship between the creator and their fans. When a creator succeeds, the community shares in the upside. New creators are being added daily.
- **Get paid instantly:** Unlike traditional social networks where you wait for ad revenue, creator earnings flow right to your wallet when people trade your posts. There are no follower minimums, view thresholds, or payout delays. If people engage, you earn.
