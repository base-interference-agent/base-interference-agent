# Base Beat: Newsletter #3

- Date: 2023-05-26
- Author: Base
- URL: https://www.base.org/blog/base-beat-newsletter-3

Action-packed weeks on Base – let’s dive in.

**The Latest News from Base**

- **Mainnet is coming**: Our eyes are set on Base mainnet. We've recently [outlined our launch criteria](https://base.mirror.xyz/gxOzGCE-9C7cNZBCo4Ubkkxr87L8BcHDfn5HViSdYGg) for builders. The checklist? Testnet stability, Optimism's successful Bedrock upgrade, and comprehensive audits. Safety and security remain paramount.
- **Code4rena audit**: We’re teaming up with Code4rena to ensure a safe transition to mainnet. We invite Wardens to join us in reviewing the code. Check out the recent [Twitter Spaces](https://twitter.com/code4rena/status/1660712574516867074) for more about what to look for, and how we’re approaching security on Base.
- **Base Camp**: Dream of mastering smart contract development? Look no further than [Base Camp](https://docs.base.org/base-camp/docs/welcome?utm_source=base_dot_org). This self-paced program takes you through development environments, token standards, and more. Earn NFTs while honing your skills and set out on your onchain journey.
- **Buzzing on Base**: From games to onchain data, Base is brimming with new dapps and services. Game developers, [Helika's rich analytics](https://twitter.com/HelikaAnalytics/status/1658105098176970753) are now at your fingertips. [DIA's integration](https://twitter.com/DIAdata_org/status/1659232151391592448) brings price oracles for tokens and NFTs. [Ethereum Attestation Service](https://twitter.com/eas_eth/status/1661735209832976389) empowers builders to decentralize identity, weave attestations into their dapps, and more. And, meet [Web3 Warriors](https://twitter.com/BuildOnBase/status/1659309788663017475), Thirdweb's game that took just three weeks to develop!

**Base All-Stars: Spotlighting Projects & Builders**

Join us on Base Discord where incredible builders share their journey. Recent highlights include:

- [Chainlink](https://chain.link/): Chainlink, a decentralized oracle platform powering many dapps, is live on Base testnet. See how you can get started [here](https://docs.base.org/tools/chainlink/?utm_source=base_dot_org).
- [Pyth](https://pyth.network/): For reliable price data integration, meet Pyth. There are over 200 price feeds available on [Base testnet](https://docs.base.org/tools/pyth?utm_source=base_dot_org).
- [Goldsky](https://goldsky.com/): Instant indexing with subgraphs available, configurable to your spec. Goldsky’s indexed.xyz dataset, a fast, open, and easy way to stream decoded blockchain data without any restrictions, is now available for [Base testnet](https://www.indexed.xyz/#).

**What’s Next?**

Stay connected with Base on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/BuildOnBase) and [Discord](http://base.org/discord) for all the latest. Keep an eye out for our meetup locations – we might be in your city soon.

We'll keep you posted on our mainnet journey. Until then, keep building!
