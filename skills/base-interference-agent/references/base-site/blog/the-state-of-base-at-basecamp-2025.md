## Exploring a network token

Until now, Base had not considered issuing a network token.

When Base launched, our priority was clear: To build a secure, low-cost, developer-friendly chain and ecosystem. Launching a token wasn’t necessary to reach those goals, and we wanted to focus on the core product.

This year, we’ve achieved our north star of sub-second, sub-cent transactions, and we’ve expanded beyond a chain into an open stack that makes it simple for anyone to build, trade, and earn onchain.

As we look toward building a global onchain economy, we believe it should be open and accessible. As the ecosystem has grown and community participation has deepened, it’s made us reconsider our philosophy. Exploring a network token is one path toward making our vision of a global onchain economy real, and a Base network token has the potential to accelerate Base’s decentralization and expand opportunities for builders and creators across the ecosystem.

We're in the early phases of our exploration, and don’t have any specifics to share around timing, design, or governance. As we consider the possibilities, we have three commitments to the community:

1. We remain deeply committed to Ethereum and will continue to build on Ethereum.
2. As a US company, we’re committed to working with regulators and legislators, and doing this right.
3. We’re committed to bringing the community along with us - we’re building in the open, listening, and learning.

If and when we move forward with a token, it will be grounded in principles, values, and in alignment with our long-term mission: to build a global economy that increases innovation, creativity, and freedom.

## What else we announced at BaseCamp 2025

At BaseCamp, we also shared major updates on how we’re helping the ecosystem move forward. Overall, our goal is to make Base the best place to build, grow, and earn.

### **Bridge between Base and Solana**

We believe Base should be a bridge, not an island. We can’t bring the world onchain if everyone is building separate, siloed ecosystems. We want Base to be a hub for the entire onchain economy — the everything chain where you can do anything — from payments, to trading, to building apps, to posting on socials.

We also believe that a global economy must be interoperable and connected. Users should be able to discover new apps, try new things, and unlock value wherever it exists, and builders should be able to reach users across chains seamlessly.

That’s why we’re excited to announce a bridge between Base and Solana.

The bridge lets you move seamlessly across chains by bridging your assets between ERC20 and SPL tokens, so you can do things like:

- Deposit and use SOL in any Base app
- Bring any Solana asset into any Base app
- Export any Base asset into Solana

This means builders can tap into more users and liquidity, and users can access the global onchain economy more easily.

The bridge is now [open-source on Github](https://github.com/base/bridge) and available on testnet, and it’ll be live on mainnet in the next few weeks.

This is just the beginning. Our goal is for Base to be an interoperable hub for the global onchain economy, and we’ll be adding support for more chains in the future.
