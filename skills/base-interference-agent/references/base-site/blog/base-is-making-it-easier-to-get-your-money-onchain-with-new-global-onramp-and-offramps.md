### **Global onramps in 190+ countries**

In 2024, Base committed to building a global onchain economy that increases innovation, creativity, and freedom. To achieve this mission, we need to help make getting onchain as easy as possible. New solutions from Binance, Bitkub in Thailand, and Chipper in Africa are examples of onramps built by others that expand access, allowing people to purchase USDC and ETH using a diverse range of payment methods tailored to their context — including local bank transfers, credit cards, and mobile payments.

With onramps now available across 190+ countries, more people than ever before can come onchain.
