# Reflections from Base Around the World

> TLDR: Base just wrapped up our first world tour. We traveled to Kenya, India, Thailand, Singapore, and the Philippines. We met over 1,000 builders, selected 50+ buildathon…

- Date: 2024-12-13
- Author: Base
- URL: https://www.base.org/blog/reflections-from-base-around-the-world

**TLDR: Base just wrapped up our first world tour. We traveled to Kenya, India, Thailand, Singapore, and the Philippines. We met over 1,000 builders, selected 50+ buildathon winners, and hosted 11 events. We’re so excited to keep working alongside our global builder community in 2025.**

**Builders are creating the onchain future, around the world**

With Base Around the World, we saw firsthand the real-world power of onchain tools in action, and met with incredible builders who are shaping the vision of a global onchain economy.

Here’s what we learned:

1. **Momentum is global.** Builders around the world are pushing the limits, and we’re here to invest in their futures. In 2025, we're doubling down on global initiatives, offering more resources, support, and office hours across time zones. We're also hiring country leads for Kenya, Argentina, and the Philippines to ensure local support is as strong as it can be. If you or someone else could be a fit, [apply here](https://www.base.org/jobs).
2. **Stablecoins are key.** To build a global onchain economy, stablecoins are a must. They power critical use cases, from cross-border remittances to local merchant payments to small business payroll. In 2025, we believe Base is uniquely positioned as the premier onchain destination for stablecoins.
3. **Local solutions, global impact.** Every region has its unique challenges, but there are common themes: control over financial lives, better access to credit, and fairer economics for creators. Builders can solve these problems locally and have a global impact.

**What’s next**

Across the globe, builders have told us they want continuous support — and we’re listening. In 2025 we’re planning to:

- **Mentorship and education:** We’re launching office hours in Africa, India, and SEA time zones to provide faster support. Plus, we’ve teamed up with [RareSkills](https://x.com/RareSkills_io) to offer an intermediate smart contract development bootcamp focused on token standards.
- **Hiring:** We’re looking for country leads in Kenya, the Philippines, and Argentina, along with more global roles in dev rel, marketing, and engineering. We will continue to grow our country coverage to ensure all builders get the support they need. Check out open positions [here](https://www.base.org/jobs).
- **Product updates:** Stablecoins remain a priority in 2025. Base believes it is uniquely positioned as the premier onchain destination for stablecoins. We’re also rolling out new product features to make it even easier to get onchain.

The Base Around the World tour was a journey we’ll never forget, and we’re excited for what’s ahead. Builders, we’ll see you in 2025.
