**The Latest News from Base**

- **The first Base testnet hardfork is coming up.** The Regolith upgrade is taking place on April 27th at 10 am PT. It is a planned upgrade to enhance the network’s security and reliability. 📡  Node operators will need to upgrade to [Base node V0.1.1](https://github.com/base-org/node/releases/tag/v0.1.1). We don’t expect any downtime or impact on the user experience. You can track the status of the upgrade on the [Base status page](https://status.base.org/).
- **Base Builder Quest is on!** Deploy a smart contract on Base & mint a commemorative NFT by artist Andre Oshea. Learn more [here](https://base.mirror.xyz/CsHm8poSS9HqWNMvPZEZDIn8LMjiNy5PwUd7z8F6G-Q), or simply get [started with the Quest](https://quests.base.org/). Follow Andre on Twitter at [@andreoshea](https://twitter.com/andreoshea?ref_src=twsrc%5Egoogle%7Ctwcamp%5Eserp%7Ctwgr%5Eauthor) or on Instagram at [@andreoshea\_](https://www.instagram.com/andreoshea_/)

**Curious Corner: Discord Q&A Gems**

A recap of topics our Discord community was most curious about. Join us for our weekly Friday Q&As in the [Base Discord](https://discord.com/invite/buildonbase) #base-stage channel.

- **What are our plans for decentralization?** We’re collaborating with Optimism to progressively decentralize Base. Our goal: Base becomes a Stage 2 rollup, per Vitalik's [vision](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/proposed-milestones-for-rollups-taking-off-training-wheels/11571). Learn more in [our blog](https://base.mirror.xyz/H_KPwV31M7OJT-THUnU7wYjOF16Sy7aWvaEr5cgHi8I).
- **How are we ensuring security on Base?** Base combines Coinbase's security expertise with an open-source foundation. We believe some of the most secure platforms are open source, with input from various teams — one great example of this is both Optimism and Base running independent security audits to strengthen and secure the same OP Stack codebase.
- **What is Base’s role in the overall L2 ecosystem?** We believe that Ethereum will scale with multiple chains, Base being one of them. Learn more about the vision for the Superchain [in our blog](https://base.mirror.xyz/H_KPwV31M7OJT-THUnU7wYjOF16Sy7aWvaEr5cgHi8I).
- **Why did we choose to build Base on Optimism?** We embrace the open-source, modular, MIT-licensed OP Stack for upgradability. We have also collaborated with the Optimism team around [EIP-4844](https://www.eip4844.com/) (proto-danksharding), and share a common vision for scaling Ethereum. Learn more in [our blog](https://base.mirror.xyz/H_KPwV31M7OJT-THUnU7wYjOF16Sy7aWvaEr5cgHi8I).
- **Is funding available for developers?** Building on Base? Submit your project for consideration to the [Base Ecosystem Fund](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeiSAod4PAbXlvvDGtHWu-GqzGpvHYfaTQR2f77AawD7GYc4Q/viewform). Need inspiration? See our [Request for Builders](https://base.mirror.xyz/lt3JR-mZ51q9eIOtGO36L3ZVzCTXmZVnbIiXz6crqzQ).
- **How are we going to reduce fees on Base?** Continuing to drive down fees on Base is a top priority — we’re working  on [EIP-4844 (proto-danksharding)](https://www.eip4844.com/) to reduce Ethereum & Base fees.
- **Are any analytics solutions available?** [Flipside](https://flipsidecrypto.xyz/hess/base-retention-BUjJWB) & [Artemis](https://app.artemis.xyz/dashboard/base) analytics available on Base testnet; many more to come on mainnet.
- **What role does community feedback play in developing Base?** Community input helps us make Base better. Share thoughts on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/buildonbase).
- **What are our plans for the gaming and NFT verticals?** Excited about the potential of NFTs and gaming to accelerate mass adoption. Our vision is to make Base an easy-to-use platform for creators & game builders.
