## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does Base provide?

Base provides settlement and access to an open onchain ecosystem. The Base team can help businesses assemble a solution using Coinbase products, third-party providers, or both.

### How do Base and Coinbase products work together?

Eligible businesses can combine Base settlement with Coinbase wallets, payment APIs, onramps, custody, and managed services. The exact architecture depends on the payment flow and operating model.

### Which currencies and markets are supported?

USDC and local-currency stablecoins are available on Base. Liquidity, redemption, onramps, offramps, and geographic coverage vary by asset and provider.

### How fast is settlement?

Base supports fast, always-available transactions. Network inclusion, confirmation, and end-to-end payment completion are different measures and depend on the selected flow and provider.

### Who handles custody and compliance?

Custody, KYB, KYC, sanctions screening, licensing, and transaction monitoring depend on the Coinbase products or third-party providers selected. Businesses remain responsible for their own obligations.

### How are payments reconciled?

Available APIs and providers can supply transaction identifiers and reporting data. Reconciliation design is scoped to the business’s ledger, custody, and payment architecture.

### Can transactions remain private?

Base Ledgers supports private transaction environments that settle on Base while keeping sensitive activity off public block explorers. Deployment and visibility depend on the selected model.

### Can Base help with prefunding?

Eligible businesses can explore financing through Coinbase products or protocols on Base. Terms, collateral, rates, and availability vary.

### How long does integration take?

Timing depends on the payment flow, providers, custody model, markets, and compliance requirements. The Base team scopes the path before implementation begins.
