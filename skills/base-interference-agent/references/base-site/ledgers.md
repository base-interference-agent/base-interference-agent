How It Works

## Keep your business private while moving money onchain.

### Settle on Base

Funds anchor directly to Base through one onchain contract, settling on Base itself rather than a separate chain.

### Unlinkable Transfers

Deposits hide the recipient. Withdrawals hide the sender. The two stay unlinkable on the public chain.

### Full Composability

Composable with the rest of Base: move funds in or out and act on them in a single transaction.

### Compliance Compatibility

KYC stays enforced at the ledger level, through Coinbase Direct or your own provider.

## Go live now, or run your own.

Start on the Ledger Coinbase operates, or stand up your own with your own custody and controls. You can begin on a managed ledger and move to self-operated with no extra work.

Powered by Coinbase Developer Platform

### Coinbase Managed

The fastest path to private transactions on Base. Coinbase runs the Ledger and the compliance, so you get confidentiality without standing up any infrastructure.

- **Atomic composability with Base.** Move money in or out in the same transaction as any onchain action, like pay-and-settle or withdraw-and-swap.
- **Confidential by default.** Senders and recipients stay hidden on the public chain; balances and transfers stay in your ledger.
- **Compliance handled.** KYC by Coinbase Direct, sanctions screening built in, and Coinbase handles licensing.
- **Nothing new to stand up.** Coinbase operates the Ledger and exposes it through Coinbase Developer Platform, so your team integrates and goes live without new infrastructure.

Self-Managed

### Partner Ledger

Your Ledger, your controls. Run your own portal and Ledger with your own setup: self-custody, your KYC provider, and your own policies.

- **Everything in Coinbase Managed +**
- **Self-custody.** Hold funds in a dedicated contract for your business. Open, auditable controls; you keep the keys.
- **Bring your own compliance.** A dedicated contract gated by your requirements, holding only your customers' funds.
- **Auditable ledger history.** Every change is proven onchain and privately. Publish the rules, and anyone can verify them.
- **Custom ledger logic.** Write programs that operate on your Ledger and prove they ran correctly without revealing anything.
