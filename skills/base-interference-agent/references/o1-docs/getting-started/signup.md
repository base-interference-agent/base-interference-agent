# Sign Up for o1 Terminal

> Quick onboarding

## Create Your Account

### Account Creation

**1. Visit o1 Terminal**

Go to [o1 Terminal](https://o1.exchange) and click the "Enter App" button

![o1 Terminal signup page](https://mintcdn.com/o1exchange/4w9GBk7uN54lPNZK/images/o1-signup.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=4w9GBk7uN54lPNZK\&q=85\&s=dbb2cf9168743cdd3e169a5f8cf1f448)

**2. Connect Your Wallet**

There are 3 signup options:

1. Gmail account
2. One time password for any email you choose
3. Wallet logins including Phantom, Backpack, MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase, and over 120 wallet options.

![o1 Terminal wallet login options](https://mintcdn.com/o1exchange/4w9GBk7uN54lPNZK/images/o1-signup-wallets.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=4w9GBk7uN54lPNZK\&q=85\&s=210911c5d1885fda269b32e991ba0897)

**3. Complete Profile**

Set up your trading profile and preferences

## Next Steps

After creating your account:

1. **Fund Your Wallet**: Ensure you have sufficient balance on supported networks

   ![Fund your wallet on o1 Terminal](https://mintcdn.com/o1exchange/h74U8tuW1ielvxuG/images/o1-fund-deposit.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=h74U8tuW1ielvxuG\&q=85\&s=f73b514c7df577930da4448dc2d51af6)
2. **Explore the Platform**: Familiarize yourself with the trading interface
3. **Join the Community**: Connect with other traders on Discord
4. **Start Trading**: Begin with small positions to get comfortable with the platform
