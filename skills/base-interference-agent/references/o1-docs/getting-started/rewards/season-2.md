# Trading Season 2: Base

> Base Season rewards and point structure

## Base Season Overview

Trading Season 2 represents the foundation of o1 Terminal's ongoing rewards program, focusing on Base chain trading with a comprehensive tier system based on ETH trading volume.

### Season Timeline

> [card] Start Date
>
> **September 5, 2025** - Season 2 begins immediately after Season 1 ends

## Base Wizard Badge 🧙‍♂️

![Base Wizard Badge](https://docs.o1.exchange/images/base-wizard-badge.gif)

Earn the exclusive Base Wizard Badge by completing one of the following requirements:

> [card] Trading Path
>
> **Trade 100 ETH** in total volume on Base chain

> [card] Referral Path
>
> **Refer 300 ETH** in total volume through your referral network

> [!NOTE]
>
> The Base Wizard Badge is a special achievement that recognizes both active traders and successful community builders. You only need to complete ONE of the two paths to earn this badge.

## Tier Benefits

Each Base Chain tier unlocks exclusive benefits and recognition:

> [card] Exclusive Badges
>
> Enter trading contest with generous bounty

> [card] Point Multipliers
>
> Higher tiers earn increased points per trade

> [card] Community Status
>
> Special recognition and leaderboard placement

> [!NOTE]
>
> Season 2 focuses exclusively on Base chain ETH trading volume. Other networks and tokens may be included in future seasons.
