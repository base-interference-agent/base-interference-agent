# o1 Doll

> The official mascot of the o1 ecosystem

![o1 doll front view: a white Matryoshka doll with a hand-drawn stick figure wearing a blue hard hat](https://mintcdn.com/o1exchange/2UHdsw33hnBBYAXM/images/o1-doll-front.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=2UHdsw33hnBBYAXM\&q=85\&s=588f0d578f25002c1bca5748efd0db2e)

The o1 doll, front view

The "o1 doll" is a stick-figure Matryoshka-style character wearing a blue hard hat. It originated as an official meme inspired by the numerous meme coins on o1 Launchpad that resemble "Basecat", an orange cat wearing a blue hard hat.

It features bouncy, playful and vivid animations, chaotic yet lively group scenes, and approachable simple hand-drawn expressions. With a fun, relaxing and highly recognizable style, o1 doll fully embodies the casual, creative and co-building atmosphere of the o1 ecosystem community.

## Design at a glance

> [card] Matryoshka silhouette
>
> A smooth, nesting-doll body in plain white with a visible seam at the waist

> [card] Blue hard hat
>
> The builder signature carried over from Basecat and the o1 Launchpad meme scene

> [card] Hand-drawn stick figure
>
> A simple black marker face, arms and legs drawn straight onto the doll

## Where it comes from

> [accordion] Basecat and the Launchpad meme scene
>
> o1 Launchpad hosts a large number of community meme coins built around "Basecat", an orange cat in a blue hard hat. The o1 doll takes that same builder hard hat and puts it on a stick figure, turning a recurring community joke into an official mascot.

> [accordion] Why a Matryoshka
>
> The nesting doll gives the character a group format by default. One doll is a single builder, a full set is the community, and the sizes make crowd scenes easy to compose without redrawing the character.
