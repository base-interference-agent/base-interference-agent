# Send and withdraw

> Send USDC or a token to someone on o1, or withdraw USDC to an external address.

Sending and withdrawing are different actions. Send pays another o1 user from the send screen. Withdraw moves USDC to an external address on a supported withdrawal network.

## Send to someone on o1

There is one send screen. You can open it in either of these ways:

* From the cash balance, choose Send, then search for an o1 user.
* From that person's profile, choose Send.

On the send screen you can send USDC, or a token you hold. Amounts under \$0.10 are currently blocked. You can add a short note. Review the recipient and the asset, then confirm.

## Withdraw USDC

Withdraw sends USDC from the Base cash balance to an address you control. Current destination networks are Solana, Base, and BNB Chain. Robinhood Chain can be used for trading, and it is not a withdrawal destination.

**1. Enter an amount**

The review screen currently shows a fee of \$0.06. Use the fee on that screen if it differs.

**2. Choose the network and address**

Paste an address or scan a QR code for the network you selected. The address format has to match that network.

**3. Confirm**

Read the destination, network, fee, and amount the recipient receives. Biometrics are requested when you have turned that control on.

> [!WARNING]
>
> Withdraw only to an address you control. Do not withdraw to Robinhood, PayPal, Venmo, or Wealthsimple. Funds sent to an unsupported destination can be lost.

Try a small withdrawal once before you move a larger balance. Fee context is also on [Fees and gas](https://docs.o1.exchange/mobile/fees-and-gas.md).

## History

History has three tabs:

* **Transfers** lists external deposits and withdrawals.
* **Cash** lists USDC movements, including sends to other o1 users, deposits, and withdrawals.
* **Trades** lists recorded buys and sells.

A swap that fails before it is recorded may not appear under Trades. Read the status screen first. See [Trading](https://docs.o1.exchange/mobile/decentralized-swaps.md).
