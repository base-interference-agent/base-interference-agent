# Trading

> Buy and sell tokens in o1 Mobile from a USDC balance on Base.

A swap in o1 Mobile starts from a token page. You choose Buy or Sell, review the quote, and confirm. The cash balance used for these routes is USDC on Base. A buy spends that balance. A sell returns proceeds to that balance. The token itself can be on any currently supported network. See [Networks](https://docs.o1.exchange/mobile/chains.md).

## Confirm a swap

**1. Open a token**

Choose it from Market, Search, Feed, a watchlist, or a profile.

**2. Choose Buy or Sell**

Enter the amount. On a buy, the amount is in USDC.

**3. Read the quote**

Check Price impact, Estimated output, Fee, and Route. Those values are for this quote, and they can change before you confirm.

**4. Confirm**

Submit the swap from your wallet. Watch the status screen for the result.

The buy screen currently requires at least \$2 in production. If the amount is too small for fees, the screen says so. Use the message on that screen if it differs from this page.

After you submit, read the status screen first. A submitted or completed swap can later be reviewed under History, on the Trades tab. Transfers and cash movements have their own History tabs. See [Send and withdraw](https://docs.o1.exchange/mobile/send-and-withdraw.md).

> [!WARNING]
>
> Quotes can expire or move with price, liquidity, and network conditions. Confirm only the quote currently on screen.

This page explains how a swap works. It is not investment advice, and market data can change before a quote is filled.

## What the route is doing

You do not bridge funds yourself, and you do not need a separate gas balance on every chain before you start. Some routes include a sponsored network fee. Sponsorship is not available for every token, network, or amount. The quote shows the costs that apply to that swap. Fee details are in [Fees and gas](https://docs.o1.exchange/mobile/fees-and-gas.md).

## Before you buy

Open the token page and check the network, contract address, liquidity, and holder information. A name or logo can be copied. Risk labels shown in the app are described in [Market and tokens](https://docs.o1.exchange/mobile/market-and-social.md).
