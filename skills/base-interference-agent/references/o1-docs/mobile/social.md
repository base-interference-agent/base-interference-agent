# Feed, friends, and profiles

> How Feed, Friends, notifications, profiles, and theses work in o1 Mobile.

Feed and Friends help you see what other people traded. Following someone adds their activity to your feed and can turn on notifications. It does not place a trade. You still open the token and confirm Buy or Sell yourself. See [Trading](https://docs.o1.exchange/mobile/decentralized-swaps.md).

## Feed

Feed has two views:

* **All** shows community activity.
* **Friends Only** shows people you follow.

Posts can include buys, sells, position updates, and theses. A post is context. Open the token page and read the current quote before you trade.

## Friends and the leaderboard

The Friends tab has two sections.

**Friends** shows My Friends and Recommended. You can follow or unfollow from a row, then open that person's profile.

**Leaderboard** ranks traders over 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, and all time. Open a trader to see their profile and positions. This is the full leaderboard. The Weekly Top Traders and Hall of Fame strips on Market are shorter 7-day and all-time cards. They are described in [Market and tokens](https://docs.o1.exchange/mobile/market-and-social.md).

## Notifications

The inbox has Unread and Read. In Settings, under Notifications, you can turn on:

* price alerts
* your holdings
* friends' activity, including a minimum trade size
* trending activity
* top traders' activity
* announcements
* new followers

On a profile you follow, the bell controls notifications for that trader. An alert is a prompt to open the token page. It is not an order.

## Profile

Your profile shows the cash balance, a portfolio chart, win rate, and a trades heatmap. Positions are split into Open and Closed. Another person's profile shows the public version of their activity, including followers, following, and positions you are allowed to see.

The heatmap, win rate, and chart describe past activity in the app. They do not predict the next trade.

## Thesis

A thesis is a short note attached to a token you hold. Current limits are:

* the account needs a token balance above zero at the time of posting
* at least 60 seconds between theses
* 200 characters or fewer

Theses are visible to other people. They are not an instruction to buy or sell.

This page is not investment advice. Social posts and leaderboard ranks can change, and they are not a promise about future results.
