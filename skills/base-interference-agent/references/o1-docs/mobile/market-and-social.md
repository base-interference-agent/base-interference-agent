# Market and tokens

> How to browse Market, search, and read a token page in o1 Mobile.

Use Market and Search to choose a token. Use the token page to check the figures the app shows before you open Buy or Sell. How to confirm the swap is on [Trading](https://docs.o1.exchange/mobile/decentralized-swaps.md).

## Market

Market can be filtered by 5 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, 6 hours, or 24 hours. The star opens your watchlist. You can add or remove a token from a watchlist on its row.

The home screen also has two short leaderboard strips:

* **Weekly Top Traders** is a 7-day list.
* **Hall of Fame** is an all-time list.

Each strip shows a short card. The full time ranges and open positions are on the Friends leaderboard, described in [Feed, friends, and profiles](https://docs.o1.exchange/mobile/social.md).

## Search

Search has Tokens and Users. You can search by token name, symbol, or address, and you can search for a person. Recent searches stay on the screen until you clear them. A low-volume control can include tokens the default results leave out.

Open a result and confirm the network and contract address before you trade. The same name can exist on more than one token.

## The token page

A token page can show:

* market cap, 24-hour volume, liquidity, and supply
* the blockchain and contract address
* holder count and the share held by the top 10 holders
* a chart with selectable ranges
* Holders, Feed, and About

About can include the description, website, and social links, plus buy and sell activity when the app has it. Feed on the token page shows related posts. Holders lists wallets the app can show for that token, including when friends hold it.

## Labels on the token

The app can flag a token before you trade. The labels currently in the product are:

* **Zero liquidity:** the creator has removed liquidity, and trading may be impossible.
* **Low liquidity:** the pool is thin. Trade carefully.
* **Low volume:** activity over the last 24 hours is low.

These labels are warnings from market data. They are not a safety rating, and they can be missing or late. This page is not investment advice, and prices can change before you confirm a quote.
