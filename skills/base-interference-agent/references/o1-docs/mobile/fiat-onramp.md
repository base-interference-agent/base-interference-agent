# Deposit

> Add USDC on Base to your o1 Mobile cash balance.

The cash balance used for buys is USDC on Base. You can add to it in two ways.

## Send USDC on Base

**1. Open Deposit**

Start from the cash balance, Market, or Settings under Deposit & Withdraw.

**2. Choose Crypto**

The app shows your Base address and a warning to send USDC on Base.

**3. Send from your other wallet**

Copy the address, send USDC on Base, and wait for the balance to update.

Check the asset, the network, and the address before you send. USDC sent on another network may not arrive in this balance.

## Continue with Coinbase

The Coinbase option opens a hosted session in the system browser. o1 Mobile asks Coinbase for a session that buys or transfers USDC to your Base address. Amount, payment method, limits, and any identity checks are chosen on Coinbase's page. o1 does not collect card details in the app.

Settings includes a Verification screen for email and phone. Opening the Coinbase session from o1 Mobile does not wait for that screen to be finished. Coinbase can still require its own checks.

> [!NOTE]
>
> Country, payment method, and limits come from Coinbase for your account. If the session fails to open, confirm that your Base address is ready and try again.

After the funds arrive, you can review a buy in [Trading](https://docs.o1.exchange/mobile/decentralized-swaps.md) or move USDC out in [Send and withdraw](https://docs.o1.exchange/mobile/send-and-withdraw.md).
