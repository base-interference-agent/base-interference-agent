## Rules
- The `multiplier` converts token units to shares of the underlying stock. One token is not always one share. Apply the current multiplier.
- Tokens are identified by address, not symbol. Symbols can be reused.
- Stock tokens have their own issuer terms and risks, and owning a token isn't owning the company's shares. Check coinbase.com/tokenize for product details.
- Trading or lending these carries market risk, and for lending, liquidation risk. Use the approval workflow in SKILL.md.
